Import Ledgers Master First

            

              Download Ledgers XML.

              In TallyPrime: Press Alt + O > Select Masters > Choose XML (Data Interchange) > Select the Ledgers XML file.

📖 Guide: Multi-Ledger / Compound Journal Entries

          How to enter 2 or more ledgers in Debit or Credit in Excel for Tally import
        

        
      


      

        
        

        💡
        

          How TallyConverter handles multi-line journal vouchers:
          

            Rows sharing the same Voucher No are automatically grouped into a single compound journal voucher in Tally. If you leave Voucher No or Date blank on continuation lines, TallyConverter automatically inherits them from the row above!

          Two Debits & One Credit (e.g. Rent & Electricity paid via Bank)
